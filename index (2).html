S2A Apps ("we", "our", or "us") provides PicWord Puzzle, a picture-and-word puzzle game. This policy explains how information may be processed when you use the app.

1. Information and game progress

The app may save game progress, completed levels, settings, and in-game coin balances on your device to support gameplay. We do not require you to create an account to play. We do not ask you to enter your name, email address, or phone number to solve puzzles.

The app may display advertisements through Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. Advertising providers may automatically process information such as device identifiers (including advertising identifiers where available), IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interactions, and technical diagnostics, subject to their policies and applicable consent choices. Ads may require an internet connection even when puzzle gameplay is available offline.

Learn about Google's practices at Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to operate game features, remember your preferences and progress, display and measure ads, detect misuse, and maintain app stability. We do not sell personal information directly to advertisers.

4. Sharing of information

Information may be processed by advertising or other service providers integrated into the app for the purposes described above. Such providers operate under their own privacy policies. We may disclose information when required by law.

5. Offline gameplay

Core picture puzzle gameplay may work without an internet connection. Some features, including advertisements, rewarded ad opportunities, external links, or app updates, require internet access.

6. Data retention and your choices

Locally stored game progress generally remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behavior. Advertising providers control retention of data they process under their own policies. You may be able to manage advertising preferences and reset or delete your device's advertising ID through Android settings. Where required, the app or advertising partners should provide consent controls.

7. Children's privacy

PicWord Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and data processing must follow applicable age-related requirements, including Google Play policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the support contact published on our Google Play Store listing.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to support the security of the app. However, no electronic system or data transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy
